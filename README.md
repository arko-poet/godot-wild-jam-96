# Ghost Charge TD

Defend the AI Hivemind from waves of vengeful human ghosts, charging at your Core for revenge.

## About
**Ghost Charge TD** was created for the [**Godot Wild Jam #96**](https://itch.io/jam/godot-wild-jam-96) in 9 days by a team of 5.

You can play the game here: [Ghost Charge TD](https://arko-poet.itch.io/ghost-charge-td)
